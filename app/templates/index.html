How it works

01

Describe it out loud

“Alpaca sweater, size M, fifteen dollars, I have three… now.” AI turns your words (and an optional photo) into a listing and publishes it.

02

Buyers race with PayPal

Your pinned link shows the drop instantly. Each payment is authorized first, then an atomic claim decides who gets a unit.

03

Winners pay, the rest don't

Winners are captured and the order is recorded under their name. Late buyers get a void: never charged, no refunds to chase.

For judges

Test it in 2 minutes

  1. Click Start a live show above.
  2. In the console, type Alpaca sweater size M, fifteen dollars, I have two in the text box and press Drop it (or use the mic in Chrome and end with “now”).
  3. Open the show link (or scan the QR) in two browsers and pay with two PayPal sandbox buyer accounts.
  4. Watch one win and the other see “You were not charged”. Open PayPal activity to see authorize, capture and void.
  5. Ask the assistant “how much have I sold?”, refund an order, then press End show for the AI recap.
